  <https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026>
- IPCC (2022). *Climate Change 2022: Mitigation of Climate Change — Summary
  for Policymakers.* <https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg3/chapter/summary-for-policymakers/>
- IPCC (2023). *Climate Change 2023: Synthesis Report.*
  <https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/syr/>
- Energy Institute (2025). *Statistical Review of World Energy* primary-energy
  series, processed by Our World in Data.
  <https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/primary-energy-cons>
- Global Carbon Project (2025). *Global Carbon Budget* fossil and industrial
  CO₂ series, processed by Our World in Data.
  <https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/annual-co2-emissions-per-country>

The introductory figures use indexed global primary-energy and fossil-CO₂
series for 2000–2024, the IEA's reported 2025 changes in global energy demand and
energy-related CO₂, IPCC AR6 direct sectoral GHG emissions for 2019, and IPCC
AR6 carbon budgets from the start of 2020. Dates and system boundaries remain
visible because these values are context, not live indicators.

The net-zero target-year map is an archival Climate Watch view retained from
the local teaching archive. It is used to motivate the gap between a political
endpoint and a quantified transition pathway. Counts are deliberately omitted,
the image is labelled archival, and current target status should be checked
against Climate Watch or Net Zero Tracker before reuse.

## Local teaching sources reviewed

The dashboard structure and examples were rebuilt from these presentations;
the archival Climate Watch map is the one directly reused visual asset:

- `04 - Background scenarios_210726.pptx`
- `ESD_PEA_course_Sacchi_280426.pptx`
- `paris_premise_intro_06_05_2026.pptx`

Their recurring material informed the IAM system map, model-coverage view,
scenario-selection criteria, sector-transformation examples, process/system
distinction and environmental trade-off chapter. Quantitative dashboard plots
use the repository's curated IAM and Brightway result files.
